Jesus Alexander Gomez Gomez

Embedded Systems Engineer · Robotics · Software Developer

Based in Mexico

Open to remote & on-site

I am an embedded systems engineer with three-plus years of professional experience in real-time systems and embedded C/C++ — most recently at Dextra Technologies (a Deloitte Consulting business) on the Verizon Connect and Continental projects, and previously at Amdocs on AT&T Mexico’s telecom backend. My day-to-day spans Yocto and embedded Linux, multi-threaded C++, CAN-bus diagnostics, and the small-but-stubborn debugging that real hardware demands.

I have been making things with electronics since 2009, when I assembled a beetle robot kit with hot glue. That habit turned into a BEng in Digital Systems & Robotics at Tec de Monterrey, a Diploma in Embedded Linux at Universidad de Guadalajara, and a growing portfolio of projects in robotics, computer vision, and bare-metal firmware. My mission is simple: build tools and solutions that improve the quality of life of the people around me — and right now, I am applying to a masters in embedded systems to push that further into real-time and aerospace work.

On this site you will find my projects, CV, and the open-source repositories I work on. The blog is where I share longer-form notes; news is where I post short updates as they happen.

Vision

I see myself contributing to safety-critical, high-reliability systems — the kind where software correctness is not optional. Aerospace, automotive, and industrial applications are where embedded systems matter most, and that is where I want to leave my mark.
