Real-time software   /   Computer vision   /   Embedded engineering

I am an embedded systems engineer working with real-time software, robotics, and computer vision.

My work spans embedded C/C++, RTOSes, embedded Linux, and CAN-bus diagnostics. I am interested in how software behaves on real hardware—and in building tools that improve the lives of the people around me.

I hold a BEng in Digital Systems & Robotics from Tec de Monterrey and am applying to master’s programs in embedded systems.
