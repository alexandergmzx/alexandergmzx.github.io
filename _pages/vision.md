## Vision

I see myself contributing to safety-critical, high-reliability systems — the kind where software correctness is not optional. Aerospace, automotive, and industrial applications are where embedded systems matter most, and that is where I want to leave my mark.

My mission is to **build tools and solutions that improve the quality of life of the people around me** — combining technical depth in embedded C/C++ and real-time systems with the broader curiosity to ask what those systems should actually do for people.

>
> — _Marconi and Tesla: Pioneers of Radio Communication_, Tim O'Shei (2008)
