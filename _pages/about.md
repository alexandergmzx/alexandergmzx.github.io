[Curriculum Vitae](/cv/)

I am an **embedded systems engineer** with three-plus years of professional experience in real-time systems and embedded C/C++ — most recently at **DXC technologies (Luxoft)** on ADAS Software development projects, previously at **Dextra Technologies (a Deloitte Consulting business)** on the Verizon Connect and Continental projects, and previously at **Amdocs** on AT&amp;T telecom backend. My day-to-day spans RTOS and embedded Linux, multi-threaded C++, CAN-bus diagnostics, and the small-but-stubborn debugging that real hardware demands.

I hold a [BEng in Digital Systems &amp; Robotics](https://tec.mx/en) from Tec de Monterrey and I am currently applying to a masters in embedded systems. My mission: **build tools and solutions that improve the quality of life of the people around me**.

On this site you will find my [blog](/blog/) is for longer-form notes; [news](#news) for short updates. For where I am headed and how I got here, see [Vision &amp; Venture](/vision/).
