My goal is simple: **build tools and solutions that increase the quality of life of the people around me**. That mission has driven everything from my first beetle robot kit in 2009 to the real-time embedded systems I work on today.

I am a confident, open-minded engineer who looks for the best in every person and environment. I combine technical depth in embedded C/C++, robotics, and real-time systems with a broader curiosity about how technology shapes the world — and where it still falls short.

## Vision

I see myself contributing to safety-critical, high-reliability systems — the kind where software correctness is not optional. Aerospace, automotive, and industrial applications are where embedded systems matter most, and that is where I want to leave my mark.
