Projects

  • SWARM, a low-cost real-time monitoring network

    2023 to present. Low-cost nodes on a shared radio schedule and a local ground station that estimates a source and directs where the next nodes go.

    • Measured: bench radio link, 10/10 link-test frames from each of two boards at desk range (3 September 2026).
    • Implemented: frame decoding, storage, provisional source estimation and the browser dashboard.
    • Simulated: a fourteen-node response, recorded. The 50 m localization and four-hour autonomy figures remain targets.
    • Seeking research supervision on the monitoring layer.
  • Parcel-sortation robot fleet

    2026 to present, design stage. Self-built tilt-tray robots on ESP32-S3 and micro-ROS with a central ROS 2 traffic controller. Design complete; simulation in progress; nothing measured yet.

  • Delta robot parallel manipulator

    2025 to present, with Armando Rodríguez. Three-DOF parallel robot in ROS 2 Jazzy: kinematics, control and an interactive browser simulator.

  • yahboomcar-ros2, mobile-robot SLAM stack

    SLAM stack for a Yahboom micro-ROS car on slam_toolbox, validated in simulation with measured gates (map quality, TF availability, sensor health). Physical SLAM not delivered.

  • fish_brain, an animatronic Big Mouth Billy Bass

    October 2025 to present. Driven from a Python host over serial to an Arduino UNO R4, with text-to-speech; a next iteration on the Arduino UNO Q, with on-board loudspeaker and controls, is in progress.

    2019, team of three, ROS Kinetic: RGB-D perception, RTAB-Map integration and custom odometry on an X80 platform.
