A fleet of small, self-built tilt-tray robots that carry parcels to their sortation chutes. Each robot runs micro-ROS on an ESP32-S3; a central ROS 2 traffic controller owns routing and right of way for the whole fleet. Started in 2026.

## Status

- **Design complete:** the robots and the traffic controller are specified.
- **Simulation in progress:** the fleet is being simulated in Isaac Sim before any hardware is built.
- **Nothing measured:** no robot has been built yet, so this page states no numbers.

This page grows when the simulation has something to show.
