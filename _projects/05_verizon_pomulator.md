At **Dextra Technologies (a Deloitte Consulting business)**, on the Verizon Connect engagement (2023 to 2024). I developed and maintained **Pomulator**, an embedded system that emulates car trips so the Android-side test stack always has rich, deterministic input.

Stack: **multi-threaded C++**, **Yocto** for the embedded Linux build, **bash** for the surrounding tooling. I added **CAN-bus diagnostics** so the emulator could retrieve and expose telemetry data otherwise locked inside the vehicle bus. Collaborated remotely with the team in **Dublin, Ireland**.
